
# Mean (Average)

## What is Mean?

The **Mean**, also called the **Average**, is one of the most common measures of central tendency.

It tells us the **average value** of a dataset.

To find the mean, we:

1. Add all the values together.
2. Divide the total by the number of values.

The mean gives us a single value that represents the **center** of the data.

---

## Formula

```text
Mean = (Sum of all observations) / (Total number of observations)
```

---

## Example

Suppose five students scored the following marks:

```text
50, 60, 70, 80, 90
```

### Step 1: Add all the marks

```text
50 + 60 + 70 + 80 + 90 = 350
```

### Step 2: Count the total number of students

```text
Number of Students = 5
```

### Step 3: Divide the total by the number of students

```text
Mean = 350 / 5

Mean = 70
```

**Answer**

```text
Mean = 70
```

---

## Outliers and Mean

An **outlier** is a value that is much larger or much smaller than the other values in the dataset.

Now suppose one student's mark is entered incorrectly as **500**.

```text
50, 60, 70, 80, 500
```

New Mean:

```text
(50 + 60 + 70 + 80 + 500) / 5

= 760 / 5

= 152
```

Notice that the average changed from **70** to **152**, even though only one value changed.

This happens because the **mean uses every value in the dataset**.

For this reason, we say:

> **The mean is affected by outliers.**


This is an excellent question because it's exactly what a data scientist asks after finding an outlier.

The answer is:

> **We don't immediately remove outliers. We first investigate why they exist.**

There are four common cases.

---

# 1. Data Entry Error (Remove or Correct)

Suppose a student's marks are:

```text
50, 60, 70, 80, 900
```

Maximum marks are only 100.

Obviously,

```text
900
```

is a mistake.

Maybe someone accidentally typed an extra zero.

✅ Action:

* Correct it if the real value is known.
* Otherwise, remove it.

---

# 2. Measurement Error (Remove)

Suppose a hospital records body temperature.

```text
36.8
37.0
36.9
120.5
37.1
```

A temperature of **120.5°C** is impossible.

The sensor probably failed.

✅ Action:

Remove or replace the value.

---

# 3. Genuine Extreme Value (Keep)

Suppose you're analyzing salaries.

```text
40k
45k
50k
48k
2,000k
```

The last salary belongs to the CEO.

It is unusual, but it is **correct**.

If your goal is to analyze **all employees**, you should keep it because it's real.

If your goal is to study **typical employee salaries**, you might analyze executives separately.

---

# 4. Business Rule

Sometimes the business decides what to do.

Example:

Netflix

One user watched

```text
950 minutes
```

After investigation, you find:

* No clicks
* Autoplay continued
* TV stayed on overnight

Business decides:

> "This is not active watching."

Instead of deleting it,

they may create a new column.

```text
possible_autoplay = True
```

Now analysts can ignore those sessions when calculating engagement.

---

# Should We Always Remove Outliers?

**No.**

Removing every outlier can be a mistake.

For example:

```text
Find Outlier
      │
      ▼
Investigate
      │
      ▼
Why is it an outlier?
      │

      │             │
Data Error?      Genuine Value?
      │             │
      ▼             ▼

      │             │
      └──────┬──────┘
             ▼
